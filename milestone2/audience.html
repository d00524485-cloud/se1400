Who We're Building For

A working profile of the primary audience, based on who's currently active in archive-fashion collecting and resale.

Geolocation

Primarily the US, UK, and Western Europe, concentrated in major fashion-forward cities — New York, Los Angeles, London, Paris, and Tokyo — where archive fashion culture and resale markets are most active.

Age

Mainly 25–40 year-olds: old enough to have real disposable income for high-end secondhand pieces, young enough to have come up through internet-native archive-fashion culture.

Gender

Currently skews male, reflecting the historically male-dominated menswear archive scene (Raf Simons, Helmut Lang, Comme des Garçons Homme), though the audience is broadening as archive womenswear from names like Galliano-era Dior and Alexander McQueen gets more attention.

Other

Fashion-industry-adjacent professionals — stylists, designers, buyers, editors — alongside serious secondhand collectors with a mindset closer to sneakerheads or watch collectors than casual thrifters. Middle-to-upper income, and willing to spend meaningfully on one well-chosen piece rather than shopping frequently.
