Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

Long-form deep dives on specific archive pieces — why a particular McQueen or Margiela piece became a "grail" — plus authentication guides for spotting fakes, designer and era primers for newcomers, a running price-watch column tracking resale values, and interviews with serious collectors.

Where will you get all of the content for your site (images & text)?

All writing is produced in-house through original research and interviews. Images come from original photography of pieces we review or collectors we profile, supplemented by properly licensed and credited archival or runway photography where original photos aren't possible.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Archive and high-end pieces carry real cultural and financial value, but trustworthy, easy-to-follow information about them is hard to find. This site helps people understand what they're looking at, what it's actually worth, and how to avoid getting burned by counterfeits.

Why should a site be built for this?

Right now, archive-fashion knowledge is scattered across Instagram captions, resale listings, and gatekeepy forum threads. There's no single organized, welcoming resource that brings history, authentication, and price context together in one place — that's the gap this site fills.

What would you like users to do at your site?

Read the deep-dive articles, subscribe to a weekly newsletter of notable new archive listings, and come back regularly to track price movement on the pieces they're watching.

What is the goal of the site?

To become the trusted, go-to resource for archive and high-end fashion — the place people check before they buy, and the friendliest entry point for anyone curious about the space but intimidated by it.
