Newsletter

One email a week. Only what matters.

Every Friday we send a short email with the archive listings worth your attention, the biggest price moves of the week, and one quick read. No spam, and you can unsubscribe any time.

Why we started it

Good archive pieces sell fast, and the listings worth buying are buried under thousands that are overpriced, mislabeled or fake. Most collectors do not have time to search several marketplaces every day. The newsletter does that searching for you, checks each piece against our authentication steps, and sends only the short list that passed. If you are new to collecting, it is also an easy way to learn which labels and seasons are worth paying attention to.

What is in each issue

  • Five listings: pieces we found for sale that are fairly priced and look authentic.
  • Price moves: the pieces from Price Watch that changed the most.
  • One short read: a new deep dive, label primer or authentication tip.
  • Reader closet: a collection sent in by a reader through our Collectors page.

A look at a sample issue

This week: Japanese outerwear

Wool coats from Yohji Yamamoto kept climbing this month, while Undercover denim cooled off slightly. We found a Junya Watanabe patchwork jacket with full tags and a seller with years of good feedback, plus a Mastermind Japan piece that is priced well below recent sales.

Our short read this week explains why Japanese labels size differently and how to check the measurements in a listing before you buy.

Questions

How often will I get emails?

Once a week, on Friday. We do not send extra promotional emails.

Is it free?

Yes. The newsletter is free and always will be.

Do you sell the pieces you feature?

No. We link to listings from other sellers and do not earn money from sales.
