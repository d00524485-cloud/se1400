How to read these numbers

Last sold is the most recent completed sale for a piece in good condition, in US dollars. Change compares that sale with the one before it. A single sale can swing the number a lot, so look at the direction over several months rather than one jump.

What moves archive prices

  • Season: pieces from famous runway seasons sell for far more than everyday basics from the same label.
  • Condition: missing tags, stains or repairs can cut the price in half.
  • Size: common sizes sell faster; very small or very large sizes can take months.
  • Attention: a museum show, a celebrity sighting or a designer leaving a label can push prices up overnight.

Use these numbers as a starting point when you negotiate, and always check a piece with our authentication guide before paying.
