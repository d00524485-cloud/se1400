Primers

Labels we cover.

A quick introduction to each label: where it comes from, what it is known for, and what collectors look for. Click any photo to see it larger, and use the buttons to filter by region.

Balenciaga

Spain/France · Founded 1917

Cristóbal Balenciaga’s couture house is known for bold volume. Recent eras swapped that for oversized streetwear, chunky sneakers and boots.

Gucci

Italy · Founded 1921

Tom Ford’s run as creative director (1994 to 2004) turned Gucci into a sleek, sexy label, and pieces from those years are still heavily collected.

Yohji Yamamoto

Japan · Paris debut 1981

Oversized, mostly black garments that drape away from the body. Wool coats and wide trousers are the classic starting points.

Undercover

Japan · Founded 1990

Jun Takahashi’s label mixes punk graphics with careful construction. Early-2000s seasons are the most wanted.

Raf Simons

Belgium · 1995 to 2022

Youth culture, music and sharp tailoring. Early-2000s bombers and parkas are some of the most valuable menswear pieces around.

Junya Watanabe

Japan · Part of Comme des Garçons

Reworked workwear, patchwork denim and technical fabrics, often made with outside brands as collaborations.

If Six Was Nine

Japan · Cult label

Rock-inspired leather jackets and heavily distressed denim. Small production runs make real pieces hard to find.

Chrome Hearts

Los Angeles · Founded 1988

Sterling silver jewelry and hardware, cross patches and heavy leather. Silver should carry a .925 stamp.

Yves Saint Laurent

France · Founded 1961

Hedi Slimane’s years at Saint Laurent (2012 to 2016) brought skinny jeans, leather and rock styling back to the runway.

Supreme

New York · Founded 1994

The skate shop that became a hype brand. Box logo pieces and collaborations drive the resale market.

Mastermind Japan

Japan · Founded 1997

Masaaki Honma’s skull and crossbones logo appears on luxury fabrics, crystals and fur-lined outerwear.

Maison Margiela

France · Founded 1988

Martin Margiela’s house is known for the split-toe Tabi boot, deconstruction and the blank white label held by four stitches.
